Supporting

At Ambucha, we believe the cultural and creative sector is what keeps things moving, it's where new ideas, new music and new images come from, and it's the world our brand feels most at home in. That's why we invest part of what we earn back into that sector.

We do this through The Gallery: our own platform where we give emerging artists a stage. We host Gallery evenings, cover the costs, and connect makers directly with buyers. Each year, we feature ten artists in The Gallery and on our socials.
